# Driftseed Isle

A tiny 3D pixel-art island farming game. Open `index.html` in any modern browser (works great on iPhone).

- **Arrive by sea:** a new game starts on a raft, drifting past islands. Every home island shares one temperate style; what makes each one unique is the land: its coastline and coves, cliffs, rivers and waterfalls, forests and meadows, all generated in full and shown in a slow flyover from the water, with a few words about what's there. Keep drifting until one feels like home, then make landfall. It's one island: no separate farm field, you farm wherever you clear.
- **A wild island, shaped by you:** you land on an untouched island: forest, thickets, rocks under the cliffs, open meadows, no roads or buildings. Pitch your tent wherever you like (that's when you name the island). Trees fall to the axe and leave stumps to dig out, and paths wear into the grass wherever you walk. The woods have a soft, cosy island look: trees with smooth, rounded crowns (sunlit on top, cool underneath, apples in summer), rounded cedars, and round bushes that flower pink in spring and blue in summer, turn rust in autumn and wear snow in winter.
- **Critters everywhere:** songbirds hop and peck in the grass, rabbits nibble at the meadow edges (white in winter), squirrels scamper round the trees, frogs sit by the river, crabs scuttle on the sand, bees drift between flowers, and now and then a deer steps out of the woods. Get too close and each one bolts its own way: birds fly off, squirrels dash up a tree, frogs plop into the water and crabs dig into the sand. They change with the time of day and the season.
- **The Island Heart:** you wash ashore with one glowing driftseed. Once your tent is up, choose where to plant it: beside your tent, up on a cliff, by the river. It sprouts on the spot, and that seed is your Island Heart. Farming, fishing, catching bugs, decorating and restoring other islands grow it from a seedling to a sapling to a great tree, with blossom from level 4 and glowing golden fruit from level 8. Each level brings something new: your boat (4), the lighthouse (8), and building kits for the general store (2), museum (5), café (6) and town hall (7), plus plots for neighbours (3, 5, 7, 9, 10, 11). You choose where each kit goes; building happens overnight, and a neighbour moves in with their house the morning after you mark out their plot. Your next goal always shows under your level; tap it to act on it.
- **Older saves** keep their fully built town.
- **Acornfield (dev):** in Settings, *Acornfield island* loads a hand-laid storybook island about ten times the size of a usual home island: a stone-paved village square round a great Island Heart tree with the town hall, store, café and museum; Cottage Lane with six neighbours' gardened cottages; a farm with fenced fields, an orchard, a windmill and beehives; striped flower gardens; a harbour with the pier; a beach campsite; and north woods on a raised plateau. Your own save is backed up first; *Restore my save* brings it back. New games stay fully procedural.
- **Just tap things:** tap a butterfly and you grab your net, creep over and catch it; tap the water to cast your rod; tap a rock, weed or stump and you use the right tool; tap a dry crop to water it, a growing one to tend it, a ripe one to harvest it (it leaps into your hands, Stardew-style), and empty soil to plant your chosen seed. Ripe crops twinkle so you can spot them. The tool bar is still there for digging new soil and for dragging a tool across a row (long-press and drag). Keys 1–7 switch tools on desktop.
- **Your look:** play as a bunny, cat, fox, dog, bear, frog, duck or penguin, in your choice of fur and outfit colour. Tap the level chip, then **Your look**.
- **Museum:** walk inside to see your catches on display. Fish swim in aquarium tanks and butterflies flutter in a glass garden. Beetles sit in terrariums, and your biggest fish turns on a spotlit plinth. Grandpa Tully, a 200-year-old sea turtle, runs the place. Outside, a few of your fish swim in the aquarium windows and butterflies visit the planters.
- **A coastal village:** each villager's home suits them: a sailor's boathouse on stilts, a dreamer's thatched round cottage, a tinkerer's brick workshop, a homebody's hill burrow, an explorer's lookout and a scholar's keeper's cottage. The harbour café sells Tailwind cocoa (faster walking and sailing) and Friendship blend (hearts grow twice as fast) for the day, and villagers stop in for morning coffee. A lighthouse on the coast sweeps its beam at night. Villagers keep daily routines: fishing, gardening, reading on benches, tinkering, exploring, singing and chatting. They also remember what you've been up to.
- **Driftseeds and restoration:** glowing driftseeds wash ashore at dawn. Every wild island starts faded, with a withered heart tree at its centre. Plant three driftseeds there and the tree grows back, colour returns to the island, and a new resident moves in. Each resident asks for a daily trade at twice the market price, so it pays to sail your harvest out.
- **Tides:** the sea rises and falls twice a day. At low tide, hermit crabs, anemones and starfish appear in the tide pools along the beach.
- **Farm:** dig with the shovel, plant with seeds, water with the can, and harvest ripe crops with any tool.
- **25 crops:** turnip, radish, carrot, lettuce, red onion, potato, cabbage, strawberry, wheat, sweet peas, bell pepper, tomato, tulip, sunflower, corn, eggplant, blueberry, pumpkin, lavender, watermelon, moonflower, grapes, peach, starfruit and night-blooming dragon fruit. They unlock as you level up.
- **Farm field (older saves):** across a wooden bridge west of home lies a big flat field, overgrown with weeds, twigs, bushes, rocks, stumps and boulders. Tap them to clear the land. Clearing gives wood, stone and fiber, plus the odd old coin or seed packet. A few weeds and twigs creep back each morning.
- **Drag-farming:** press and hold a tile, then drag across others to till, plant, water, tend, harvest or clear a whole row in one motion. The action is chosen from the first tile you touch.
- **Sell:** tap the shipping bin by your home, or open the Bag. One crop sells for 1.5× each day.
- **Rare variants:** crops can ripen as Giant, Moonlit (only at night), Golden, Crystal or Prismatic. Collect them all in the Almanac.
- **Between harvests:** tap the sea to fish (wait for the bobber to plunge, then tap), pick up beach finds and messages in bottles, catch butterflies and moths, pull weeds, shoo crows off your crops, and tend each crop once a day for a growth boost.
- **Orders:** three new requests arrive each morning; finish them all for a bonus and a Mystery Seed.
- **Explore:** board your sailboat at the dock and sail to procedurally generated islands (meadow, tropical, pine, autumn, snowy, volcanic and swamp). Each has its own bugs, wild plants, berries, beach treasures and fish. Use the Sea Chart to navigate; the world curves away at the horizon like a rolling log.
- **Travel:** the islands sit close together, a few seconds' sail apart. Tap any island you've already found on the Sea Chart to travel there instantly. Sail out to discover new ones. At sea, scoop up floating crates, driftwood and bottles, and watch dolphins leap alongside the boat.
- **Islandex:** the main goal: record every fish, bug, plant and find in the archipelago (100+ entries), plus rare crop variants at home.
- **Fishing (Animal Crossing style):** fish shadows swim near every shore and out at sea. Their size hints at the species, and big ones show a fin. Cast near a shadow, wait while it nibbles, then tap the moment the bobber is pulled under. Tap too early and it gets spooked.
- **Islands and rivers:** wild islands are 20% bigger. Five huge Great Isles sit at the edge of the archipelago, with three levels of cliffs. Each has one or two wide rivers that run from an inland pond down to the sea, dropping over waterfalls at each cliff and crossed by wooden bridges. The volcano isle has a river of lava. Larger normal islands have a narrow stream. Your villager walks around rivers and crosses at the bridges. Tap a river to fish for 10 new river fish, including trout, koi, steelhead, arowana and the rare Golden Trout. Your home island can now be expanded twice more.
- **Island style:** bigger islands with cliffs and raised plateaus, rounder trees, in the style of Wild World. Sailing uses real pathfinding, so the boat steers around islands.
- **Painted ground & gradient trees:** grass, paths, sand and cliffs use patterned textures, as in Animal Crossing, not thousands of grass blades, which keeps the polygon count low. Round trees are clusters of drooping leaves shaded light to dark, and pines have layered, gradient boughs. Beaches slope gently down into the water, and four kinds of palm grow on them: coconut, tall leaning, short fan and twin-trunk palms. The villager, boat, fish, houses and rocks are all more detailed.
- **Atmosphere:** waves lapping at every shore, footprints and footsteps in the sand, drifting clouds, the moon and stars, petals, autumn leaves, snow, embers or spores depending on the island, swamp mist, rain that stops mid-day and leaves a rainbow, shooting stars you can wish on (star fragments wash up the next morning), meteor showers, and a gentle music box melody with birdsong by day and crickets at night.
- **Dev tools** (tap your level badge): time-of-day slider, time speed (pause to 60×), weather, shooting stars and meteor showers, skip to morning, extra shells, and **Showcase farm**. It loads a fully built late-game farm: the field grown to full size and fenced, a stone avenue and cross path lined with lanterns and tulip planters, packed sprinkler plots of vegetables, an orchard and grape trellises round a windmill, striped flower beds with beehives, and sunflowers along the fence. It also gives a Villa, top tools, max level, every island found and restored, and a full museum. Your own save is backed up first, and **Restore my save** brings it back.
- **Build:** buy decor and useful buildings (sprinklers, beehives, lucky clover, windmill), expand the island, and upgrade your tent to a villa.
- **Real time and real seasons:** the island keeps your clock, like Animal Crossing. The time of day is your device's time, a new day starts at 6am, and crops grow over real hours (a turnip in about 3, dragon fruit in about a day). While you're away, time still passes: crops keep growing and each morning comes. The trees follow the calendar: blossom in spring, deep green in summer, reds and golds in autumn, and bare branches, snowy pines and snow on the ground in winter. Dev tools can run the clock faster, return to real time, or preview any season.
- **Controls:** drag to spin, pinch to zoom, two fingers to pan. Progress saves on your device.

Built with three.js (r128). Everything is rendered at low resolution, then passed through an outline and dither shader for the pixel look.

## Development

The game is built from `src/` into the single file `index.html`. See [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) for the module map, conventions and how to add content.

```
npm run build   # src/ → index.html (also fails on syntax errors)
npm test        # build, then a headless play-through (needs Playwright)
npm run check   # CI: is index.html up to date?
```

Open `index.html?debug` to get `window.DS`, a small console API for testing.

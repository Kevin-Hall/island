# Driftseed Isle: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [date]_

Driftseed Isle is a game you play on your own device. It doesn't collect, store or share any personal information.

- **No data collection.** The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services, and no accounts.
- **No internet.** The app doesn't connect to the internet. Everything it needs is inside the app.
- **Your save stays with you.** Your game progress is stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage. It may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, which Apple manages under your own settings. Deleting the app deletes your save.
- **Children.** Because the app collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

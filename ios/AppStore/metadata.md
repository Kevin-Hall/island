## Description (4000)
Wash ashore with nothing but a raft and one glowing driftseed.

Drift past islands until one feels like home, then make landfall on an untouched wild island of forest, meadow, cliffs, rivers and waterfalls. Pitch your tent wherever you like, name your island, and plant your driftseed. It grows into your Island Heart, and everything you do helps it grow.

FARM AND FORAGE
• 25 crops to grow over real time, from turnips to night-blooming dragon fruit, with rare Giant, Moonlit, Golden, Crystal and Prismatic harvests
• Till, plant, water and harvest a whole row in one swipe
• Every morning the island puts out mushrooms, berries, acorns, clams and dig spots to find
• Dig up wildflowers roots and all, and replant them anywhere

FISH, CATCH AND COLLECT
• Cast for fish whose shadows swim along every shore and out at sea
• Catch butterflies, moths and beetles, and creep up on the wildlife
• Fill the Islandex with over 100 fish, bugs, plants, finds and wild creatures

SAIL AND EXPLORE
• Board your sailboat to find tropical, pine, autumn, snowy, volcanic and swamp islands, each its own little ecosystem
• Bring faded islands back to life with driftseeds, and new neighbours move in

A LIVING ISLAND
• Real clock and real seasons: blossom in spring, gold in autumn, snow in winter
• Tides, rain and rainbows, shooting stars and meteor showers
• Villagers with their own homes, routines and memories of what you've done together

MAKE IT YOURS
• Dress Sprig with hats, outfits, faces and accessories, or play as a bunny, cat, fox and more
• Build, decorate your home, and choose your island's look and tree style
• The Island Fair holds a new contest every day: win ribbons and trophy cups

No ads, no in-app purchases, no account and no internet needed. Your island saves on your device.

## Notes for App Review
Driftseed Isle is a complete, offline 3D game. All of its code, art, fonts and sound are bundled in the app and rendered with WebGL inside a WKWebView. Nothing is downloaded at runtime: the web view only loads files from the app bundle through a custom URL scheme (driftseed://), and the app makes no network requests.

To get started: tap "Keep drifting" to look at islands, then "Make landfall" to choose one. Follow the on-screen goals: pitch your tent, plant your driftseed, then farm, fish and explore. No login is required.

Native features: progress is saved locally and backed up to Application Support, sound respects the ring/silent switch, and the game plays haptic feedback.
